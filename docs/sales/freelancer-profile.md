# Freelancer Profile

**Status:** Phase 2 working master profile  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-27

## 1. Purpose

This document is the canonical source for the business's freelancer profile.

Platform-specific profiles may adapt length, formatting, and emphasis, but they must remain factually consistent with this document, the approved business positioning, the service catalog, and verified portfolio evidence.

## 2. Profile identity

The public profile should identify the business as a professional web-development practice focused on building, modernizing, and maintaining practical business web experiences.

Do not add personal credentials, years of experience, certifications, named clients, platform ratings, technology claims, or performance results until they are supplied and verified.

## 3. Headline direction

Use a clear service-and-problem headline rather than a generic title.

Approved positioning source:

**Build, modernize, and maintain business websites and web applications.**

Platform-specific versions may add a concise audience or specialization reference when supported by the approved positioning.

## 4. Master profile summary

**Draft master summary:**

I build, modernize, and maintain business websites and focused web applications with clear scope, practical usability, and maintainable implementation.

I work across business websites, landing pages, website redesign and modernization, custom web applications, dashboards and portals, and website maintenance and improvements.

Projects are approached around the actual business need: establishing a professional web presence, making an existing site clearer and more usable, improving important user flows, or implementing focused functionality beyond a standard marketing website.

I aim to keep scope clear, communicate the work plainly, and deliver the agreed web solution without making unsupported promises about traffic, leads, revenue, or conversions.

## 5. Services to present

Use the approved service catalog as the source:

- Business websites
- Landing pages
- Website redesign and modernization
- Custom web applications
- Dashboards and portals
- Website maintenance and improvements

A platform profile should emphasize only the services relevant to the platform's character limit and target audience.

## 6. Audience

The profile can address:

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Startup founders and early-stage companies
- Consultants and professional-service businesses
- Agencies needing additional web-development capacity
- SaaS founders and product teams
- Businesses with outdated, confusing, slow, or poorly maintained websites

The final platform version may narrow this audience based on the platform and verified acquisition evidence.

## 7. Differentiation themes

Use observable delivery characteristics rather than superlatives:

- Clear scope and deliverables
- Modern, responsive implementation
- Practical usability and important user flows
- Maintainable implementation
- Appropriate attention to accessibility, performance, security, and integrations when relevant
- Structured handover and documentation
- Honest representation of capabilities and results

These are positioning themes, not proof of completed outcomes. Portfolio evidence should be added as it becomes available.

## 8. Proof section

Proof should be added only from verified sources.

Eligible proof includes:

- Real client projects that are approved for representation
- Founder-owned work clearly labeled as Founder-owned
- Demonstration work clearly labeled as demonstration
- Approved portfolio screenshots
- Public project links
- Verified client feedback with permission
- Verified project measurements

Do not publish placeholder proof as though it were completed evidence.

## 9. Calls to action

Suitable early calls to action include:

- Discuss your website project
- Request a project estimate
- Review your website needs
- Discuss a redesign
- Discuss a custom web application

The final conversion flow will be defined as the Sales System matures.

## 10. Claims control

The profile must not claim or imply:

- Guaranteed leads
- Guaranteed conversions
- Guaranteed revenue
- Guaranteed SEO rankings
- Best, #1, or world-class status without authoritative evidence
- Client results that have not been verified
- Qualifications or certifications that have not been documented
- Client names without permission
- Experience or project counts that have not been verified

## 11. Platform adaptation rule

Before creating a platform-specific profile:

1. Start from this master profile.
2. Confirm every factual claim against the approved source documents.
3. Adapt the wording to the platform's format and audience.
4. Add only verified proof.
5. Keep pricing out unless the Founder approves the relevant pricing policy.
6. Record material platform-specific changes in GitHub.

## 12. Review checklist

- [ ] Headline matches approved positioning.
- [ ] Services match the approved service catalog.
- [ ] Audience description matches approved positioning.
- [ ] No unverified credentials or experience claims are included.
- [ ] No fabricated client work or results are included.
- [ ] Proof is linked to verified portfolio evidence where used.
- [ ] No confidential client information is included.
- [ ] No pricing policy is implied or invented.
- [ ] Call to action is concrete and appropriate to the platform.
- [ ] Platform-specific version remains traceable to this master profile.
