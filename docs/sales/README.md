# Sales System

Phase 2 contains the reusable material used to present the business, qualify opportunities, prepare proposals, and track leads.

## Current components

- freelancer-profile.md — canonical master profile and profile-source rules
- proposal-templates.md — canonical proposal structure and service-specific prompts
- qualification-flow.md — canonical lead qualification process
- lead-tracker.md — planned lead tracking structure

## Authority and evidence

Sales material must remain consistent with the approved business positioning, service catalog, portfolio evidence, and Founder decisions.

Never invent:

- client work
- years of experience
- qualifications or certifications
- client results
- testimonials
- revenue or conversion results
- platform ratings
- named clients without permission

Platform-specific profile versions should be derived from the canonical freelancer profile rather than becoming independent sources of truth.

Proposals must derive scope from qualified client requirements and the approved service definitions. Commercial amounts must come only from Founder-approved pricing.

Qualification should establish sufficient clarity before proposal preparation without inventing requirements, pricing, deadlines, or client commitments.

## Workflow

Positioning → Master Profile → Platform Variant → Proposal → Qualification → Lead Record

The master freelancer profile is the source for reusable public positioning. Platform-specific wording may be shortened or reformatted without changing the underlying facts.

The proposal system converts a qualified need into a bounded scope, deliverables, assumptions, acceptance conditions, and approved commercial terms.

The qualification system determines whether an opportunity is ready for proposal, needs clarification, is not a fit, or should remain undecided.
