# Business Plan — Initial Foundation

## Objective

Build a sustainable freelance web-development business focused on modern websites and web applications.

## Initial acquisition channel

Freelancer.com is the initial marketplace acquisition channel. The longer-term objective is to diversify acquisition into direct and repeat business so the marketplace is not the sole source of revenue.

## Initial client profile

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Startup founders
- Consultants and professional services
- Agencies needing development capacity
- SaaS founders
- Owners of outdated or poorly functioning websites

## Initial services

1. Business websites
2. Landing pages
3. Website redesign and modernization
4. Custom web applications
5. Maintenance and improvements

## Business principles

- Sell clear outcomes and deliverables.
- Prefer sustainable scope over maximum project volume.
- Build proof through honest portfolio work and completed client work.
- Use completed projects to improve the business system.
- Increase pricing as evidence, specialization, and delivery capability improve.

## Foundation target

Establish the business operating system before moving into public portfolio-site implementation.
