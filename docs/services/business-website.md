# Business Website

## Purpose

Build a professional, responsive website that clearly presents an organization's identity, services, credibility, and next steps.

## Ideal client / use case

Best suited to:

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Consultants and professional-service businesses
- Startup founders establishing a credible web presence
- Organizations replacing an incomplete or outdated basic website

## Core deliverables

Typical deliverables include:

- Agreed page structure and navigation
- Responsive page layouts for agreed breakpoints
- Home page and agreed core business pages
- Clear calls to action appropriate to the site
- Contact or enquiry flow when included
- Reusable visual/components structure
- Basic metadata and page-level technical configuration appropriate to the project
- Production-ready implementation
- Handover notes for agreed editable content or operating tasks

## Typical scope

A standard business website commonly includes a small set of marketing/information pages such as:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Service detail pages where required
- Contact
- FAQ or other agreed supporting pages

The exact page count and content structure must be agreed before implementation.

## Boundaries / exclusions

Not included unless explicitly scoped:

- Full brand identity creation
- Extensive copywriting or content strategy
- Professional photography/video
- Complex customer accounts
- Custom business workflows
- Advanced dashboards or portals
- E-commerce
- Ongoing SEO campaigns
- Paid marketing
- Large-scale content migration
- Ongoing maintenance after handover

## Optional extensions

Possible project-specific extensions:

- Additional pages
- Blog or resource section
- CMS/content editing workflow
- Analytics configuration
- Accessibility improvements beyond the agreed baseline
- Performance optimization beyond the agreed baseline
- Approved third-party integrations
- Multilingual support
- Deployment assistance

## Client inputs

The client should provide:

- Business information and approved service descriptions
- Brand assets or visual direction
- Required pages
- Contact details and preferred contact method
- Approved legal/privacy content where required
- Domain/hosting access when deployment is included

## Acceptance criteria

The website is complete when:

1. All agreed pages are implemented.
2. Navigation and agreed user flows work as defined.
3. Responsive behavior meets the agreed device/breakpoint expectations.
4. Approved content and assets are placed correctly.
5. In-scope forms or integrations function as agreed.
6. No known critical in-scope defects remain.
7. Agreed handover material is provided.

Business results such as traffic, leads, rankings, or revenue are not acceptance criteria unless separately and explicitly defined as a measurable deliverable the business controls.
