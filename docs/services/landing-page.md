# Landing Page

## Purpose

Create a focused page that communicates one offer, audience, campaign, or desired action clearly.

## Ideal client / use case

Suitable for:

- A specific service or product offer
- A launch or announcement
- A campaign or acquisition page
- A startup needing a focused initial web presence
- A business testing a clearer offer before expanding a full website

## Core deliverables

Typical deliverables include:

- Focused page structure
- Hero/value proposition section
- Supporting sections based on the agreed message
- Trust/proof elements where evidence exists
- Clear primary call to action
- Responsive implementation
- Form or enquiry interaction when included
- Basic page metadata and technical configuration appropriate to the project

## Typical scope

A landing page usually centers on one primary objective and one main conversion path.

The page may include:

- Headline and supporting proposition
- Benefits or feature explanation
- Problem/solution framing
- Social proof or evidence
- Process or offer details
- FAQ
- Final call to action

The exact content hierarchy is agreed before implementation.

## Boundaries / exclusions

Not included unless explicitly scoped:

- Full multi-page website
- Brand strategy or identity development
- Ad campaign management
- Guaranteed conversion rate
- Guaranteed lead volume
- Paid copywriting or professional content production
- Complex account or application workflows

## Optional extensions

- Multiple landing-page variants
- Approved analytics/event tracking
- Form integration
- CRM or email integration
- A/B testing support where the relevant infrastructure exists
- Supporting thank-you or confirmation page

## Client inputs

The client should provide:

- Target audience
- Offer or campaign objective
- Desired call to action
- Approved messaging/assets
- Proof or testimonials that can legally be used
- Form destination or lead-handling requirements where applicable

## Acceptance criteria

The page is complete when:

1. The agreed primary offer and audience are clearly represented.
2. The agreed page sections are implemented.
3. The main call to action works as defined.
4. Responsive behavior meets the agreed expectations.
5. Approved assets and content are correctly presented.
6. In-scope integrations function as agreed.
7. No known critical in-scope defects remain.

Conversion or revenue outcomes are not guaranteed by the landing-page service.
