# Business Plan — Initial Foundation

## Objective

Build a sustainable freelance web-development business centered on modern business websites and web applications.

## Initial Acquisition Channel

Freelancer.com is the initial marketplace acquisition channel.

The longer-term objective is to diversify acquisition into direct and repeat business so that the marketplace is not the sole source of revenue.

## Initial Client Profile

Primary targets:

- small and medium-sized businesses
- startup founders
- consultants and professional services
- agencies needing development capacity
- SaaS founders
- owners of outdated or poorly functioning websites

## Initial Services

1. Business websites
2. Landing pages
3. Website redesign and modernization
4. Custom web applications
5. Maintenance and improvements

## Business Principles

- Sell clear outcomes and deliverables.
- Prefer sustainable scope over maximum project volume.
- Build proof through honest portfolio work and completed client work.
- Use every completed project to improve the operating system.
- Increase pricing as evidence, specialization, and delivery capability improve.

## 30-Day Foundation Target

Establish the business operating system, publish the initial portfolio foundation, update the Freelancer profile, and begin targeted client acquisition.
